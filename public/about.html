Our Story

Since 2012, we've been bringing children in from the cold.

It started with one child.

In 2012, [describe the moment — the child you met, where you found them, what made you stop]. It wasn't a big plan. It wasn't an organisation yet. It was just a handful of us who looked at a situation that felt hopeless and decided it wasn't.

We pooled what we had. We asked friends. We knocked on doors. And somehow, that one child had a meal that night and a place to sleep.

Then there was another. And another.

The work we actually do

Over the years, we've taken in hundreds of street children. We've fed them. We've clothed them. We've gotten them medical care when they were sick. And when we could, we've reunited them with family — or given them something family-shaped when there was none to return to.

But feeding a child for a day is not the same as giving them a future. So we took a bigger step: we started a small home — a place where children who have nowhere else to go can live, sleep safely, and be looked after by people who know their names.

Sometimes a child just needs a meal. Sometimes they need a shirt that fits. Sometimes they need a bed. We take whatever form help comes in — money, food, clothes, or time. Our doors are open to all of it.

That home is the heart of what we do now. It is small. It is modest. But it is ours, and it is theirs.

What we're building now

A home is not enough. A child who lives with us still needs an education. Right now, the children in our care either walk long distances to over-crowded schools, or they don't go at all.

We are currently building a school — proper classrooms for the children in our home and for children in the surrounding community. This is the biggest thing we have ever attempted. It is also the most important.

Every classroom we build is another group of children who get to learn to read, to write, to do math, to dream about a life that isn't just survival. That's the whole point.

A home keeps them safe. A school gives them a future.

Why we do this the way we do

We've watched too many well-meaning donations disappear into overheads, or get delayed for months, or end up somewhere nobody can point to. So we made a rule from day one: every shilling goes to a child, and every child has a face.

That means:

  • We work directly with families, schools, and local leaders — not through layers of intermediaries.
  • We publish what we raise and what we spent it on.
  • If a campaign can't be completed, the money goes back to the general fund and gets used for the next child in line.
  • Our team is made up of people from the same communities we serve. Nobody's a stranger here.

What we've done so far

Some of the children we brought in years ago are now in secondary school. A few have come back to volunteer. That, more than any number, is what tells us we're doing something right.

Why we're asking now

The need hasn't gone away. If anything, it's grown. Every week, we meet another child on the street who has nowhere to sleep. Every week, we get requests we can't fulfil — a child who needs surgery, a family about to lose their home, a bright student whose fees are due next month.

And now, there's a school to build.

We can't help all of them. But we can help the next one. And the one after that.

That's what this platform is for.

So if you're reading this and wondering whether your donation matters — it does. It becomes school shoes. It becomes a hospital bill. It becomes a meal. It becomes a classroom. It becomes a child who gets to stay in school for one more term, and then one more after that.

It becomes a story like the one we started with in 2012.

Thank you for being part of it.

— The KidsFund Team
