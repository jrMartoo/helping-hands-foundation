What Is Helping Hands Foundation's Child Sponsorship Programme in Kenya?

Helping Hands Foundation's child sponsorship programme connects generous individuals, families, churches and organisations around the world directly with a named child in Helping Hands Foundation's care in Kangundo, Machakos County — 60 km east of Nairobi.

Unlike general charity donations, child sponsorship is personal. You are matched with a specific child. You receive their story, their photograph and regular updates on how your support is changing their life. Your child knows who you are. Many sponsors and children build meaningful long-term relationships that last well beyond the child's time at Helping Hands Foundation.

Helping Hands Foundation's child sponsorship programme in Kenya is fully managed, transparent and accountable. All funds are directed to the child's direct costs of care and are reported on regularly.

What Does Your Child Sponsorship Cover?

When you sponsor a child in Kenya through Helping Hands Foundation, your monthly gift covers the full cost of that child's care, including:

Food and Nutrition

Three nutritious meals per day, daily snacks and access to clean drinking water from Helping Hands Foundation's on-site borehole. Good nutrition is the foundation of a child's physical and cognitive development — and for children managing HIV/AIDS, it is medically critical.

Safe Shelter and Daily Care

A secure, clean dormitory, bedding, clothing, personal hygiene supplies and 24-hour pastoral care from Helping Hands Foundation's trained residential staff. For many children in our programme, this is the first stable home they have ever known.

Education at Dhamini School

School fees, uniforms, exercise books, stationery and all learning materials for enrolment at Dhamini School — Helping Hands Foundation's on-site primary school in Kangundo. Every sponsored child attends school every day. Your sponsorship ensures that poverty or ill health is never a barrier to education.

Healthcare and HIV/AIDS Treatment

Full medical care at St. Hannah Community Clinic, including routine health monitoring, ARV treatment for children living with HIV, vaccinations, dental care and emergency medical support. For HIV-positive children, consistent access to ARV medication is life-sustaining — your sponsorship makes it possible.

Psychosocial Support and Counselling

Individual and group counselling sessions, therapeutic activities and mentorship from Helping Hands Foundation's trained psychosocial staff. Many children in our care carry the weight of bereavement, trauma and HIV-related stigma. Your sponsorship funds the emotional care that helps them heal.

Extracurricular Activities and Enrichment

Music, acrobatics, dance, arts and sports — activities that build confidence, creativity, teamwork and joy. At Helping Hands Foundation, we believe childhood should be more than survival. Your sponsorship gives children the chance to discover their talents and simply be children.

How to Sponsor a Child in Kenya Through Helping Hands Foundation

Sponsoring a child through Helping Hands Foundation is straightforward, transparent and takes just a few minutes to set up.

  1. Choose your sponsorship level

    Decide how much you would like to give monthly and which aspect of care you would like to fund — or opt for full sponsorship to cover everything.

  2. Register your sponsorship

    Complete the sponsorship form at helpinghandsfoundation.org/sponsor-a-child or email info@helpinghandsfoundation.org with your name, contact details and chosen sponsorship level.

  3. Be matched with your child

    Helping Hands Foundation will match you with a child whose needs align with your sponsorship level. You will receive your child's profile — their name, age, photograph, story and a note about their life at Helping Hands Foundation.

  4. Set up your monthly gift

    Pay via M-Pesa, bank transfer, PayPal or card payment. Helping Hands Foundation's finance team will confirm receipt of every payment and issue regular acknowledgements.

  5. Stay connected

    Receive termly updates on your sponsored child — their school progress, health milestones, activities and life at Helping Hands Foundation. Write to your child if you wish. Some sponsors visit Helping Hands Foundation in person to meet their child — you are always welcome.

Who Are the Children You Will Be Sponsoring?

The children at Helping Hands Foundation come from some of the most difficult circumstances in Kenya. They include:

Children orphaned by HIV/AIDS

Children who have lost one or both parents to AIDS and who have no family member able to care for them. Many arrived at Helping Hands Foundation malnourished, unschooled and without access to the ARV medication they need to survive.

Children living with HIV

Children who were born with HIV or contracted it in infancy and who require consistent medical management, specialist care and a stigma-free environment in order to live full, healthy lives.

Children from Nairobi's informal settlements

Children rescued from situations of neglect, abuse or extreme poverty in areas including Kibera, Mathare, Mukuru, Korogocho and Eastlands — brought to Helping Hands Foundation when no safer option existed in the city.

Children from across Machakos County

Children from Kangundo, Mavoko, Athi River, Mwala and surrounding areas who were identified by social workers, area chiefs or community members as being at acute risk.

Children who fell through the system

Children excluded from government OVC programmes due to HIV status, administrative barriers or simply being missed. Helping Hands Foundation accepts those whom no other institution will take.

What Happens When Your Sponsored Child Leaves Helping Hands Foundation?

Helping Hands Foundation's goal is not permanent institutionalisation — it is to prepare every child for a safe, independent and fulfilled life. When a sponsored child is ready to leave Helping Hands Foundation, the centre works to ensure a strong transition, which may include:

  • Family reintegration — Returning to a rehabilitated family member who has received parenting support, economic empowerment and follow-up from Helping Hands Foundation's community team
  • Secondary school placement — Supporting older children to transition to secondary school, including boarding school sponsorship where needed
  • Vocational training — Connecting young adults with skills training and livelihood opportunities in Nairobi and Machakos County
  • Community follow-up — Maintaining contact and support after leaving to ensure the child does not return to a situation of vulnerability

Your sponsorship does not just cover a child's time at Helping Hands Foundation. It builds the foundation for everything that comes after.

Why Sponsor a Child Through Helping Hands Foundation Kenya?

There are many child sponsorship programmes in Kenya. Here is why Helping Hands Foundation is the right choice:

  • Fully registered and accountable — Helping Hands Foundation is a registered CCI under Kenya's Children's Act. Your funds are managed transparently and accounted for in full.
  • Direct impact — Your sponsorship is matched to a specific named child. You always know exactly where your money is going and who it is helping.
  • Specialist HIV care — Helping Hands Foundation is one of very few children's homes in Kenya providing residential HIV/AIDS specialist care. Your sponsorship supports children that most institutions will not accept.
  • Locally led, nearly 20 years of experience — Founded by Kenyan Christine Wambui in 2005, Helping Hands Foundation is deeply rooted in the community it serves. No overhead-heavy international bureaucracy — just direct, expert, local child welfare.
  • A relationship, not just a transaction — Helping Hands Foundation's sponsorship model is built on genuine connection between sponsor and child. You are not just giving money. You are becoming part of a child's story.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I choose which child I sponsor in Kenya?

Helping Hands Foundation does its best to match sponsors with children based on your preferences — age, gender or particular circumstances. However, final matching is determined by Helping Hands Foundation to ensure equity across all children in care. Contact info@helpinghandsfoundation.org to discuss your preferences.

How often will I receive updates on my sponsored child?

Sponsors receive a full update every school term — three times per year — including a written report on the child's academic progress, health status and life at Helping Hands Foundation, plus a recent photograph. Additional updates are shared at special milestones such as school graduation.

Can I write to or visit my sponsored child in Kenya?

Yes. Helping Hands Foundation encourages communication between sponsors and children, subject to child safeguarding guidelines. Letters, cards and small gifts are welcome. Sponsors wishing to visit Helping Hands Foundation in Kangundo should contact info@helpinghandsfoundation.org to arrange a visit in advance.

What happens if my sponsored child leaves Helping Hands Foundation?

If a child is reintegrated with family or transitions to secondary school during your sponsorship period, Helping Hands Foundation will notify you and offer you the opportunity to continue sponsoring another child or to redirect your gift to Helping Hands Foundation's general care fund.

Is my child sponsorship tax deductible?

This depends on your country of residence. Helping Hands Foundation can provide documentation of your giving for tax purposes where applicable. Contact info@helpinghandsfoundation.org for a donation receipt or letter of acknowledgement.

Can a church or organisation sponsor multiple children in Kenya?

Yes. Group and institutional sponsorship is warmly welcomed. Churches, schools, businesses and community organisations can sponsor one or more children together. Helping Hands Foundation will provide tailored reporting and recognition for group sponsors. Contact info@helpinghandsfoundation.org to set up a group sponsorship plan.

How do I know my money is being used correctly?

Helping Hands Foundation is a registered CCI and operates with full financial transparency. Sponsors receive itemised updates on how their contribution is spent. Helping Hands Foundation's accounts are available to review and the centre welcomes donor visits at any time by appointment.
