What to Bring — and What Not To

We accept most things in usable condition. To keep the children safe and our storage manageable, we cannot accept:

Please do not bring:

  • Expired or opened food items
  • Broken, torn, or heavily worn clothing
  • Underwear or socks that have been used
  • Electronics that don't work
  • Furniture we haven't agreed to accept beforehand

What Happens to Your Donation

  1. You drop off the items (or we arrange pickup for large donations).
  2. We log every item we receive — what it was, when it came, who gave it.
  3. We distribute it directly to the children in our home, or to families in the community.
  4. We share updates and photos so you can see where your gift went.
